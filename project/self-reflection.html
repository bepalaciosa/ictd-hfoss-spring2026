Self-Reflection

CS491/591 • Spring 2026 • University of New Mexico

< back to syllabus

Due: Finals Week, Thursday 5/14, 11:59 PM
Format: Short-answer responses on Canvas Quizzes
Weight: Half of the Project Diary (7.5% of overall course grade)

This is an individual assignment and your responses are private: only the instructor will read them. Please be honest and reflective. This is not a place to perform, rather it is a space to look back on your semester genuinely, share how things went, and say anything you did not have another opportunity to say. You will not be penalized for anything you share here.

Format

This will be a quiz assignment on Canvas with four (4) short-answer questions. Guidance on length is provided for each question. Please be thoughtful but succinct.

Questions

  1. Reflect on your experience in this course over the semester as a whole. What did you get out of it? What was rewarding, and what was hard? (5-10 sentences)
  2. How much work did you personally put into the group project? Be honest about the time and effort you dedicated, what you are proud of, and where you fell short. (5-10 sentences)
  3. How did your teammates contribute to the project? This response is anonymous and only the instructor will read it. (3-5 sentences)
  4. Is there anything else you would like the instructor to know, whether about your experience, your circumstances this semester, or anything you did not have another opportunity to share? (3-5 sentences, or type "N/A" if there is nothing to add)

Grading

The self-reflection is graded on completion and is worth 12 points. Responses must meet the minimum length guidance for each question to receive full credit.

Your self-reflection score is combined with your five Progress Log scores to make up your Project Diary grade. See the late work policy for penalty details.

Submission

Submit directly in the Canvas quiz, no file upload needed.

Please remember this is an individual assignment. Do not share your responses with teammates.
